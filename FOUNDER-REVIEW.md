# Yugrow Founder Review

> Not engineering. Not code. Not bugs. A weekly check on the product itself.
> Answer these questions every week. Archive each review in `reviews/founder/`.

---

## Week [Number] — [Date]

### What did we build?

*List the meaningful outputs — not tasks, but what actually changed for the product.*

### What surprised us?

*What was unexpected? A user behaviour, a technical constraint, a market signal, a personal realization.*

### What assumptions were wrong?

*Something we believed that turned out to be incorrect. This is the most valuable section.*

### What did users teach us?

*Even if it's just one person — what did watching someone use Yugrow reveal?*

### What should we stop building?

*Features, abstractions, or polish that don't serve the current milestone.*

### What became more important?

*Something that moved up in priority unexpectedly.*

### What Founder Decisions changed?

*Were any FD's revised, superseded, or newly created?*

### Should anything be frozen?

*Is any subsystem stable enough to never redesign?*

### Are we still solving the same problem?

*The most important question. If the answer is no, everything above needs re-evaluation.*
