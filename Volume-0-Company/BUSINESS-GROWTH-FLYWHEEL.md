# Business Growth Flywheel

> **Yugrow is organized around the lifecycle of a business, not around software categories.**

---

## The Flywheel

```
       ┌─────────────┐
       │   Attract   │
       └──────┬──────┘
              ↓
       ┌─────────────┐
       │   Convert   │
       └──────┬──────┘
              ↓
       ┌─────────────┐
       │    Sell     │
       └──────┬──────┘
              ↓
       ┌─────────────┐
       │   Deliver   │
       └──────┬──────┘
              ↓
       ┌─────────────┐
       │   Support   │
       └──────┬──────┘
              ↓
       ┌─────────────┐
       │   Manage    │
       └──────┬──────┘
              ↓
       ┌─────────────┐
       │  Analyze    │
       └──────┬──────┘
              ↓
       ┌─────────────┐
       │    Grow     │
       └─────────────┘
```

Each stage feeds the next. Data flows naturally. AI improves every stage.

## Stage to Product Mapping

| Stage | Customer Question | Products | External Category |
|-------|------------------|----------|-------------------|
| Attract | "How do people find me?" | Sites, SEO, Social, AI Content | Get Customers |
| Convert | "How do I turn visitors into leads?" | CRM, Funnels, WhatsApp, Chat, Forms | Get Customers |
| Sell | "How do I close deals?" | Pipeline, Quotes, Proposals, Automation | Close Deals |
| Deliver | "How do I deliver work?" | Projects, DMS, Automation | Close Deals |
| Support | "How do I keep customers happy?" | Helpdesk, KB, AI Assist, Live Chat | Support Customers |
| Manage | "How do I run the business?" | Books, HR, Inventory, Attendance, Contracts | Run Operations |
| Analyze | "How is my business performing?" | Dashboards, AI Insights, Reports | Scale with AI |
| Grow | "How do I scale?" | CheckIN, Marketplace, AI Agents, Events | Grow Your Network |

## Design Principles

1. **Data flows naturally between stages** — No manual data entry between functions
2. **AI improves every stage** — From content generation to lead scoring to predictive analytics
3. **The flywheel is self-reinforcing** — More customers → More data → Better AI → Better outcomes
4. **Not every business needs every stage** — Capabilities are modular; the platform adapts

## Decision Framework

Before building any feature, ask: *Which stage of the flywheel does this serve?* If the answer is none, don't build it.
